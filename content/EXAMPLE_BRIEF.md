# Fictional product used in every example: Fieldnote

One product, used consistently across all 61 workshop examples, so a reader who opens three boards sees one story.

## The product
**Fieldnote** is a mobile + web app for small HVAC and plumbing maintenance companies (5 to 60 technicians). Technicians log interventions from the field (photos, parts used, time, customer signature); dispatchers plan the day and see who is where; the owner gets invoicing data without retyping.

## People
- **Léa**, 34, field technician, 8 interventions a day, phone in a gloved hand, hates typing, currently fills a paper sheet in the van at 7pm.
- **Marc**, 45, dispatcher, one screen with a whiteboard behind him, spends the morning on the phone rerouting technicians when a job overruns.
- **Sophie**, 51, owner of a 22-technician company, does invoicing on Sunday from photos of paper sheets, loses about 6% of billable parts.
- **Karim**, 29, product manager at Fieldnote. **Ana**, designer. **Tom**, tech lead. **Nadia**, head of sales. **Julien**, support lead. **Élise**, CEO / founder.

## Facts you can reuse
- Founded 3 years ago, 140 paying companies, 2 100 technician seats, ARR 1.1 M€, net revenue retention 104 %.
- Pricing: 29 € per technician per month.
- Activation problem: 38 % of new companies never get a technician to log 10 interventions in the first 2 weeks.
- Retention: companies that connect their invoicing tool in month 1 churn at 4 % a year, the others at 19 %.
- Competitors: Jobber (generalist, US), Synchroteam (older, feature-heavy), paper + Excel (the real one).
- Roadmap candidates that come up everywhere: offline mode, parts catalogue with barcode scan, customer portal, automatic route planning, invoicing integration (Pennylane, Sage), photo annotations, technician skills matrix, recurring maintenance contracts.
- North Star candidate: interventions logged in the field the same day.
- Team: 2 squads (Field app, Office web), 9 engineers, 1 PM, 1 designer, 1 data analyst. A third squad (Integrations) is planned.
- Big recent incident: a release broke photo upload for 2 days in March; 40 support tickets.
- Quarter objective under discussion: cut time-to-first-10-interventions from 14 days to 5.

## Stage framing
The same product can be shown at different stages depending on the workshop: pre-PMF examples talk about the first 10 customers and paper sheets; scale-up examples talk about 2 squads, dependencies and the Integrations squad; mature examples talk about the recurring contracts line and legacy sync code. Pick the stage that fits the workshop naturally.

## Tone
Concrete, short sticky-note style items (3 to 10 words each). Real-sounding quotes from Léa, Marc, Sophie. Numbers from the facts above. No em-dash. French version: same content, natural French, not word for word.
