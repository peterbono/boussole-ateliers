Preparation, timed agenda, pitfalls

Alignment & delivery

Pre-mortem

Agenda · 1 h
5 min
Set the scene
Say it exactly like this: 'It is six months from now. The project failed badly. Not delayed, failed.' Do not soften it.
10 min
Write the causes
Everyone writes, in silence, one cause per sticky. Push for volume: at least 5 per person. Nobody speaks.
15 min
Read around the room
Go around, one sticky per person per turn, until all are on the board. No comment, no defense, no 'that would never happen'.
15 min
Group and vote
Cluster similar causes. Each person gets 3 dots for the causes they find most likely. Take the top 5.
15 min
Mitigate
For each of the top 5: what do we change in the plan this week? One action, one owner, one date. Write it in the Mitigations column.
Before
  • The whole project team plus the sponsor. The sponsor must stay for the full hour, not just the opening.
  • Send the project plan, or the one-page brief, 2 days before. Everyone must know what is supposed to happen.
  • Prepare three columns on the board: Causes of failure, Grouping, Mitigations. Sticky notes ready, one color for everyone.
Pitfalls
  • Do not run it without the sponsor. The most useful causes are the ones only the sponsor can fix, and they need to hear them said.
  • Stop anyone who argues against a cause during the read-around. The exercise works because nobody is allowed to defend the plan yet.
  • Do not leave with 12 mitigations. Five with owners beat twelve without.
